Veitch of North Sinton
Ashkirk Parish, Roxburghshire

 

1(iii). John Veitch of North Sinton. John received a grant of the lands of Sinton from his brother Alexander in exchange for those of Easter Dawyck, and founded the branch of the Veitches of North Sinton. He was succeeded by his son George, who in 1525 had the lands confirmed to him by William Veitch of Dawyck, who held the superiority, and who in the following year entered Walter, the son of George, in them. (Hist.Peeb)
