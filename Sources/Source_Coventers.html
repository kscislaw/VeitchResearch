Index of Covenanters

A period of religious upheaval that begin about 1638 in opposition to reforms of the Scottish Presbyterian litergy. The Covenanters signed a pledge adhereing to the old litergy. They controlled parliament by 1640 and sent armies to support Parliamentarians in England, which then led to a Scottish civil war in 1644-47. Defeated by Cromwell in 1650s. Declared unlawful by Charless II in 1662.
