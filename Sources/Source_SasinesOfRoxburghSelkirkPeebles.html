Sasines of Roxburgh, Selkirk and Peebles
(source: "Genealogical memoranda on the surname of Veitch, containing pedigree of Veitch of Glen")


1610

5 May 1618 Sasine in favour of John Vaitch in Clerklands, of Walter Vaitch of North Sinton's lands of Clerklands (occupied by said John) lying in shire of Roxburgh, following on a precept of sasine by said Walter Vaitch of North Sinton. Wit: Georgis et Andrea Vaitches filius legittimus dicti Joannis (and others). Fol: 84g

18 Oct 1618 (fol. 144) - Sasine in favour of sr Alexr Grege minister at the churchof Drummelzier and Agnes Vaitche his spouse of Thos Porteous of Glenkirke's lands of Glenquhone called the Glencothe also the Glenhigden, in the paroch of Glenquhone shire of Peebles

1620

12 Mar 1620 (fol. 210) - Sasine dated 15 Feb in favour of Mr Alexr Greg minister at Drummelzier and Agnes Vaiche his spouse etc.

24 Aug 1620 to which Malcolm Vaiche servitor of Sir John Stewart of Traquair is a witness - concerning the lands of Edgerstoun, shire of Roxburgh

1 Aug 1623 (fol. 176b) Sasine dated 26 July in favour of Malcolm Veache brother germain of William Veache of Dawick (following on a precept of sasine by Sir William Makdowgall of Makarstoun, knight) of the lands and barony of Makarstoun in the parcel of the same, shire of Roxburgh.

1630

10 Nov 1630 - Patrik Vaich in Peibles his seasing of fourtie merke furth of sevin rode of land in Peibles grantit be George Ingles - dated 10 Nov 1630 (fol. 405-6)

4 July 1631 - Jonet Vaich spous to Wm Vaich in Peibles her seasing of land in Peibles grantit be him to her (fol. 439)

20 Jan 1632 - Alexander Vaich elder his seasing of Maner alias Castlehill and Jonet Gedes his spous, grantit be Johne Lowis in Maner with consent of Isobell Scot his spous, daitit 12 Jan. 1632 (fol. 473)

12 Jan 1632 Another sasine by the said John Lowis with consent of his spouse in favour of said Alexr Vaich and his spouse of said lands dated 12 Jan (fol. 473-4)

18 May 1632 Sasine of James Williamson and Wm Vaich notar, burgesses of Peebles of the vicarage Kirklands of the Kirke and paroch of Peebles - dated 12 May (fol. 11g)

1 Dec 1635 (fol. 274) Sasine dated 28 Nov of the lands of Quhythauch [Whitehaugh] in the paroch of Peebles in favour of Rebecca Vaich daughter of Patrick Vaich sheriff clerk of Peebles nad spouse of Thomas Tweedie of Quhythauch - in liferent etc.

1 Dec 1635 (fol. 274g) Sasine in fulfilment of a marriage contract made between Thomas Tweedie of Quhythauch (Whitehaugh] on the one part and Patrick Vaich sheriff clerk of Peebles and Elizth Vaich his spouse for themselves and tabeing the grenden on him the said Patrick for Rebecca Vaich his daughter and she with consent of her said parents etc on the other part, dated at Peebles 20 Oct 1635. Whereby the said Rebecca is seised in liferent of the said Thomas Tweedie's lands of Frankislands etc in the paroch Peebles.

[betw 15 Jul and 10 Aug] 1636 (Vol iv fol. 308) Sasine dated July 9 following on a charter and precept by James Poterorne of Caverhill to Cristine Vaiche his spouse and lawful daughter of umq John Vaiche of Dawik in liferent of said Jas Paterson's lands of Caverhill and 1/3 part of Cylack etc. Wit: Alexander Vaiche in nether horsbrugh fratre germanus dictis Bristinea etc

17 Nov 1636 (fol. 337) Sasine dated 28 Oct of John Vaich fier of Dawik [Robt Vaich ye lawl son of William Vaich of Dawik acting as his attorney etc] of Walter Scot of Glenrath's ¼ part of Glenrath and other lands

15 Feb 1637 (fol. 345b) Sasine dated 10 Feb of Alexr Vaich in Nether Horsbrug'. Comeared Wm Scot in Marchdyke baille in that part to John Lowis of Manor etc and Alexr Vaich in Nether Horbru' [Horsburgh] together with Wm Vaich notar burgess of Peebles as attorney of Alexr Vaich son of said Alexr 'natar maxim' [the elder] and there said Alexr Vaich, elder having and holding in his hands a precept etc made by the said John Lowis of Manor with advice and consent of Dr Theodore Hay rector of Peebles to the said Alexander Vaich senior in liferent and the said Alexr Vaich his son in fie etc of all and hail the mains of Manor alias Castlehill in vic. Peebles etc.

15 Feb 1637 (fol. 346) Sasine dated 10 Feb of the aforesaid Alexr Vaich senior in liferent and Alexr Vaich his son etc upon a charter by the said John Lowes with consent aforesaid of the lands of Welschhous in the lordship of Hundilshope vic. Peebles

22 Nov 1637 (vol. 389b) Sasine dated Nov 13 of Alexr Vaich in Nether Horsburgh ['de Dawik' in the margin] in liferent and Alexr Vaich, younger, his son in fee (upon a charter etc by John Lowes of Manor with consent of Isobel Scot his spouse and Mr Theodore Hay rector of Peebles) of the lands of Welshous in the paroch of Manor vic. Peebles. [It appears Alexr Vaitch the elder has the right to redeem the said lands from his son on payment to him or his heris etc …without consent of his son.]

22 Nov 1637 (fol. 390-92b) Sasine dated Nov 13 of the said Alexr Vaich in Nether Horsburgh in liferent and Alexr Vaich his son in fie, upon a precept of Sasine under the great seal of those parts of ½ of the lands and barony of Manor called the mains or Castlehill of Manor etc, Halmanor etc, Hairhope, an acre called the Wodhous aiker in the paroch of Manor vic Peebles, formerly pertaining to John Lowis of Manor, held in capita etc other lands etc.

22 Nov 1637 (f. --) Sasine dated Nov 13 1637 compeared Alexr Vaich in Nether Horsburgh upon the ground of his lands underwritten and there for the love etc he has and bears toward his spouse Margaret Scot as well for the fulfilment of his part of a contract of marriage made between him the sd Alexr on the one part and John Scot of Hundilishope for himself and taking the burden on him for the said Margaret Scot his sister germain and the said Margaret for herself etc on the other part dated 28 Oct 1635, gave liferent sasine of an annual rent of 640 merkes to be uplifted forth of his, the said Alexander's lands of Halmaner etc Hairhope lying in the barony of Manor vic Peebles. [In the margin: "Sasinae Margaretaie Scot spousae Alexr Vaich de Dawik"]

22 Aug 1638 (fol. 419) Sasine dated 12 July Thomas Tweedie of Whitehaugh and Rebecca Vaich his spouse of the lands of Bonyngtoun pertaining to John Bulle

14 Feb 1639 (fol. 429b) Sasine dated 5 Jan of Patrick Vaich sheriff clerk of Peebles and Elizh Vaich his spouse and to the heir of their bodies etc of 7 roods of land lying in divers parts etc. Alexr Vaich of Manor being Baillie in that part for John, Earl of Traquair.

1640

1640 Sept 25 (fol. 479b) Sasine dated 8 Sept of William vaich notar public, burgess of Peebles and Jonet Vaich his spouse of 3 roods of the Kirklands of Peebles

1642 Dec 20 (fol. 549) Sasine dated 19 Nov of Patrick Vaich sheriff clerk of Peebles and Elizabeth Vaich his spouse in conj[ ] liferent etc of 5 roods of the Kirklands of Peebles

1643 Feb ult (fol 572) Sasine dated 7 Feb of Helen Vaich daughter lawful of the deceased George Vaich of Clairkland and now spouse of James Vaich appearing of North Sinton, (following on a precept of sasine made by Sir John Vaich of Dawick knt superior of the lands following) as nearest heir of her said father lawfully procreat between him and Isobel Riddell his spouse and of all and hail the lands of Clerklands in vic. Roxburgh. Wit: Andro Vaich in Clerklands Thoma Vaich ibidem.

1650

Robert Mcdougall her uncle of lands in the barony of McCairstoun; also the nether mains thereof sometime possessed by the deceased Malcolm Vaiche, thereafter by John Charteris etc etc.

1660

1665 Jan 20 (fol 365b) Sasine dated Jan 13 of Wm Veatch servitor to James Thorbrand carpenter in Hawick of a house there granted by Margaret Thornbrand one of the heirs portioners of deceased Thos Thornbrand burgess of Hawick with consent of Adam Inglis her spouse.
