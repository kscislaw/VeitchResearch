1554 [pg 369] [John Vache renounces lands at Corslie in favor of Walter Vache] Corslie. Quam prius habuit quondam Johannes Vache, avus [grandfather] Walteri Vache de North Sintoun, in feodifirma, ut patet in rentali de data apud Edinburgh, octavo die mensis Aprilis, anno 1510, et post ejus dicessum Johannes Vache, patruus [uncle] dicti Walteri, obtinuit infeofamentum ejusdem de data apud Edinburgh, die decimotertio mensis Aprilis, anno 1581, quod infeofamentum factum fuit dicto Johanni in prejudicium dicti Walteri, veri heredis ejusdem, in minoritate domini regis et deveniebat sub ejus generali revocatione, et ideo dietus Johannes renunciavit easdem terras in favorem dicti Walteri tanquam nepotis et veri heredis dieti quondam Johannis, pront in quodam contractu inito et confecto inter dictos Johannem et Walterum de data prescripta, registrato in libris consilii supremo domine nostre regine, latius eavetur, et quia jure hereditario sibi Waltero pertinent dicte terre, assedantur de novo dicto Waltero Vache, heredi predicto, in feodifirma, solvendo annuatim pro

Google translation: That he had once Johannes Vache, grandfather Walter Vache, North Sintoun the farm, as is the rental of data at Edinburgh, on the eighth day of April, 1510, after its dicessum Johannes Vache, an uncle said Walter it has been infeftment same date at Edinburgh, on the thirteenth of April 1581, which infeftment, it has been said John prejudice said Walter, the true heir of the minority of the king and he come under its general withdrawal and, therefore, the said Johannes report to those lands; in favor of the said Walter like a grandchild and a true heir of the said deceased John prone to some contract concluded between John and Walter data afore Registered in the books plan last of our Lady wider eaves, and because right hereditary Walter belong said land , tack on the new Walter Vache, heirs in fee-paying annually instead
