31 October, 1694. - The magistrats and counsell of Peebles, considdering that ther is ane call offered to the presbiterie of Peebles be the Duke of Queensberrie, and
some others of the heritors of Peebles, in favors of Mr. Alexander Orrock, minister of Hawick, to be minister of Peebles, contrair to thair inclinationes, the man being altogether unfitt for this parish, as they are informed be persons of good reputation, therfor the saids magistrats and counsell doe commissionat and appoynt Robert Forrester, present proveist, and John Tueiddy, ane of the present baillies, to repair to the presbiterie of Pebles at Kirkurd upon the second day of November nixt, and ther to labour quhat they can to hinder the said Mr. Orrock to be imposed upon this parish ; and incaice the presbitrie offer to proceed, to appeall from them to the commission of the kirk, to quhom it wes recommended be the generall assembly to plant the kirk of Peebles at the transportation of Mr. William Veitch, or to the said generall assembly, quhich of them sitts first, and at sitting aither of the commission of the kirk or generall assembly, to appear before them, and to doe quhat they can to hinder the place from being imposed upon ; as also to consult some able lawer quhat the toun can doe for obtaining a modified stipend to whoever sail be minister, suteable to the teynds of the parish, and to doe everything requisit and necessary thairanent, or anent any other of the tounes affaires quhich requyr advyse to be taken of a lawer ; and ordaines the clerk to subscryve this commissioun in name of the counsell.
