Manor Parish Register Peeblesshire 1663 to 1695

1679 Mar 22 - Issobell daut of John Veach in Kirkhop baptized

1680 May 18 - Christian Veach spouse to James Patorsone of Caverhill was buried

[blank from 1680 to 1685]

Burials

1678 [no date] David Veatch son to Wm Veatch buried

Marriages

1678 Dec 20 - James Williamson and Jennet Veitch gave up yr names for ordination in order to marriag etc and were married 16 Jan 1679

1685 Aug 27 - James Veitch and helen Noble in Dawik was married at the church of Manner who brought a testimonial wt yr etc

1685 Nov 13 - John Veitch in the parish of Dawik and Bessie Thomason in the parish of Peebles wes married at Manner Kirk.
